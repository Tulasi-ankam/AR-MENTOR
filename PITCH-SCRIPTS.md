**[1:30–2:15] DEEP TECHNICAL INSIGHT**

"Let me tell you what makes this technically novel — not just educationally.

Our AR engine is pure WebGL — Three.js running in a browser. No ARKit. No ARCore. No Unity. This means it works on 99% of smartphones, including ₹8,000 Android devices that 80% of Indian students own.

The Emotion Detection Engine is our most original invention. We don't use facial recognition — which would require a camera permission most students won't grant. Instead, we analyze behavioral signals: touch frequency, scroll patterns, replay count, question density. These signals feed into a classifier that detects confusion with 78% accuracy in our pilot. When confused, the system doesn't just simplify text — it changes the AR rendering mode, the color palette, and the narration speed.

The OCR-to-AR pipeline runs in under 3 seconds. Tesseract.js processes the image client-side. The extracted text hits our AI orchestrator which selects the right 3D model, generates the explanation hierarchy (simple/normal/advanced), and returns a full AR scene configuration. The student never touches a setting.

The Knowledge Cosmos is built on a graph database concept mapped to a 3D force-directed layout. Each node is a topic. Edges are conceptual connections. As mastery grows, nodes grow brighter. This visual shows the student exactly where they are in their learning universe."
